At checkpoint 1 we had a problem statement and an empty repo. The repo now contains the runtime, two fault-injection experiments with two baselines, generated results, and three findings.

1. Exactly-once is a property of the target, not the client; tier 3's honest guarantee is at-most-once with surfaced ambiguity.

3. Premise granularity is a dial with a floor: file hashes over-refuse; symbols land benign edits but miss meaning changes.
